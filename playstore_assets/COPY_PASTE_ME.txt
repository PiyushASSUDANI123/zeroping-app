ZeroPing is the ultimate zero-cloud, 100% offline peer-to-peer (P2P) communication and high-speed file-sharing app engineered for privacy, speed, and freedom.

No internet? No cellular tower? No Wi-Fi router? No problem.
ZeroPing connects you directly to nearby devices using direct Wi-Fi Hotspot, Wi-Fi Direct, and Bluetooth RFCOMM technology with zero latency and zero data consumption.

🔥 TOP FEATURES YOU'LL LOVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🎙️ WALKIE-TALKIE MODE (Voice Notes over Bluetooth)
• Push-to-talk live voice notes streamed over RFCOMM.
• Military-grade voice messaging without any internet or cell reception.
• Crystal-clear offline audio playback.

🥷 STEALTH MODE (Panic Disguise)
• Quick double-tap on the app bar instantly transforms your chat into a fully working fake Calculator!
• Keeps background Bluetooth socket & audio streams active while masking your screen.
• Long-press '=' or double-tap top bar to safely return to chat.

👻 GHOST MESSAGES (Self-Destructing Text)
• Send secret, encrypted messages with 5s, 10s, or 30s timers.
• Receiver must tap-and-hold to reveal the secret.
• Automatically purged from memory and permanently wiped from local storage upon expiration.

🎮 IN-CHAT MINI GAMES (Zero-Latency)
• Play embedded interactive Tic-Tac-Toe right inside your chat bubble!
• Sub-millisecond state updates streamed directly between devices.
• 100% offline gaming with zero lag.

⚡ ULTRA-FAST FILE & MEME SHARING (100 MB/s)
• Share 4K movies, heavy apps, ZIP files, and photos at blistering speeds.
• Smart 10KB sequential chunking protocol ensures large meme/image transfers never crash low-bandwidth connections.
• Visual real-time progress bars with auto-assembly.

🔒 MILITARY-GRADE AES-256 ENCRYPTION
• End-to-end encrypted chats, voice notes, and file transfers using AES-256 CBC.
• Symmetric keys are derived locally on-device. No plain text ever leaks over the air.

🛡️ ZERO CLOUD, ZERO TRACKING, 100% PRIVATE
• No phone number, no email, and no sign-up required.
• No cloud servers, no databases, and zero tracking SDKs.
• What happens on your device stays on your device.
