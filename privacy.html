1. Overview & Core Philosophy

ZeroPing is an offline, peer-to-peer (P2P) wireless file sharing and walkie-talkie communication application engineered for privacy, speed, and resilience. Our fundamental core philosophy is simple:

Zero-Server, Zero-Cloud Architecture

ZeroPing operates 100% offline using direct peer-to-peer Wi-Fi Hotspot, Wi-Fi Direct, and Bluetooth RFCOMM sockets. We do not operate any user-tracking servers, cloud databases, external analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or third-party telemetry. Your data never touches the internet.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect exactly ZERO personal data:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register with an email address, phone number, password, or social login.
  • No Telemetry or Tracking: We do not track device unique hardware identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, Android ID) or personal usage habits.
  • No Cloud Storage: Messages, files, voice notes, and avatars are stored strictly inside your device's sandbox local storage.
  • No Third-Party Analytics or Ads: There are no Google AdMob, Facebook Analytics, Firebase Tracking, or advertising trackers embedded in the application.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

To discover nearby devices and transfer data offline without an internet connection, ZeroPing requests only the minimal necessary Android permissions:

Permission Purpose Data Handling
BLUETOOTH_SCAN
BLUETOOTH_CONNECT
BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE		 Required on Android 12+ to discover nearby peer devices, establish local RFCOMM chat sockets, and stream encrypted voice notes. Used strictly with neverForLocation flag. No location is gathered, stored, or transmitted.
NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION		 Android operating system requirement to scan for nearby Wi-Fi Direct and local hotspot nodes. Used solely by Android's internal Wi-Fi stack. ZeroPing never records, tracks, or shares your geographic GPS coordinates.
RECORD_AUDIO Required solely for the user-initiated "Walkie-Talkie Push-to-Talk" feature. Audio is captured only while holding the record button, encrypted locally with AES-256, transmitted directly to the connected peer, and never stored on any remote cloud.
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES
READ_MEDIA_VIDEO
READ_MEDIA_AUDIO		 Allows users to selectively choose files, images, and memes to send over the local P2P network. Only files explicitly selected by the user are read and streamed. We never scan or index your media library.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Keeps large local file transfers and walkie-talkie audio streaming active when the screen turns off or the app is minimized. Displays an active notification to the user while background transfer is in progress.

4. Military-Grade Security & Encryption

All communications transmitted over ZeroPing (text chat, ghost messages, audio voice notes, and memes) are secured using AES-256 CBC symmetric encryption. Encryption keys and initialization vectors (IVs) are deterministically derived locally on both devices from the shared room identifier. No unencrypted plain text is broadcast over the air.

5. Ghost Messages & Data Destruction

ZeroPing includes a "Ghost Message" feature designed for self-destructing text:

  • Users configure a countdown timer (5 seconds, 10 seconds, or 30 seconds).
  • The recipient must tap-and-hold to view the message content.
  • Upon timer expiry, the message is permanently wiped from RAM and deleted from local storage on both devices.

6. Children's Privacy

ZeroPing complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Since the app collects no personal information whatsoever, it does not knowingly collect, store, or solicit information from children under the age of 13.
